Before you renovate

Your renovation questions, answered.

A little clarity upfront makes planning easier. Confirm project-specific details in your written agreement.

Do I need a permit for my renovation?

Permit requirements depend on the work and your municipality. Structural changes, new or altered plumbing, and some basement renovations can require permits. Before starting, confirm which approvals apply and who will obtain them. For Toronto projects, see the City of Toronto permit guidance.

How long will the renovation take?

Timing depends on scope, existing conditions, approvals, material lead times, and trade availability. Share your preferred dates during the consultation and ask for a project-specific schedule. Any changes to the scope should be reviewed for their effect on timing.

Is a deposit required?

Deposit amounts and payment milestones are project-specific. Confirm them in the written estimate or contract before authorizing work. Ask which payments relate to materials, progress milestones, and final completion; do not assume a standard percentage.

Who chooses and purchases the materials?

Discuss your preferred finishes, budget, and any materials you already have. The written scope should identify who selects, orders, pays for, and receives each item, along with allowances and how substitutions will be approved.

Do you use subcontractors?

Trade arrangements depend on the scope. Ask which work Prime Reno will perform directly and whether specialist trades or subcontractors will be involved. Confirm who coordinates and supervises each part of your project before work begins.

What should I send when requesting a quote?

Include your city, the rooms or work involved, approximate dimensions, photos if available, preferred timing, and budget priorities. Call 289 600 10 16 or email Prime Reno to arrange the next step.
